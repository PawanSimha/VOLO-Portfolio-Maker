# VOLO Portfolio Builder

VOLO is a powerful, modern, and easy-to-use portfolio builder designed for developers, designers, and creatives.

## Features

- **Dynamic Templates**: Choose from a variety of high-end, responsive templates.
- **Real-time Preview**: See your changes as you make them.
- **Easy Customization**: Update your bio, projects, education, and social links with ease.
- **Export to Web**: Generate a clean, production-ready HTML/CSS portfolio in seconds.

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Fill in your professional details in the sidebar.
3. Select a template and preview your site.
4. Click **Export Portfolio** to download your custom website.

## Technologies Used

- HTML5 / CSS3
- JavaScript (Vanilla)
- Google Fonts (Outfit, Inter, etc.)
- Lucide Icons
